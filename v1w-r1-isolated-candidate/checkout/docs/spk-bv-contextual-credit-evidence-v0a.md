# SPK Contextual Credit Evidence v0a

**Status:** research candidate, offline and read-only. **Not** a freeze, learned policy, causal-effect theorem, program promotion, hardware mapping, or claim of increased cognition.

**Fixed ancestor:** annotated tag `spk-architecture-bounded-reference-v1`, commit `5af99cd1f4d603bf0c8e1557a6561c107765fdb5`. Requires the separate, previously supplied *Back Validation Contract v0* research package. All frozen SPK references remain unchanged.

## 1. Research question and hypothesis

The goal is **not** to make upstream neurons obedient to downstream approval. It is to learn whether a context-conditioned program change can improve the network's independently measured task utility **after** counting exploration, execution, memory and feedback costs.

For a fixed target neuron `i`, a pre-treatment context class `c`, a baseline local program `p0` and a candidate `p1`, define the *target causal estimand*:

`Delta_i(c) = E[U | do(Pi_i=p1), C_pre=c] - E[U | do(Pi_i=p0), C_pre=c]`.

This is a **research estimand**, not a quantity computed by BV v0a. A comparable paired simulator replay may approximate it only if the **same** initial state, external input, fixed non-target programs, topology, observation and utility policies are used, with state reset and independent audit of the actual runs. `contextual_credit_v0a.py` checks only the structure of externally supplied data and computes paired descriptive diagnostics. Hashes are not attestations; the fixture is entirely synthetic.

The null hypothesis is no gain over fixed-program, central-search, random-search and shuffled-feedback controls at **matched total cost**. Positive feedback from neighbors is not evidence of global gain.

## 2. Three separate sources of information

| Channel | What is allowed | What it does **not** establish |
|---|---|---|
| O: observation | A destination's reported, versioned metric and abstention | Truth, calibrated confidence, utility or marginal credit |
| H: contribution hypothesis | A claimed causal ancestry path, context and specific local program change | That an ancestor's program *caused* the effect |
| V: controlled verification | Independently measured task utility on matched simulator replays | Real-world causality, hardware speed, universal generalization |

**No write authority** is granted by any of O, H or V. `EVIDENCE_FOR_FURTHER_VERIFICATION` is a research label, **not** A4c staging, approval, CAS, epoch change or promotion.

## 3. Paired intervention protocol

For each trial `k`, two **externally produced** rollouts shall differ in **exactly one intended variable**, the target neuron-local program:

- baseline: `Pi_i = p0`
- candidate: `Pi_i = p1`

Both share declared (`context_id`, `seed`, `initial_state_sha256`, `input_sha256`), declared topology and policy digests, and frozen baseline commit. Context features are declared to be *pre-treatment*: do not condition on consequences of the candidate's execution. Each trial arm records global task score `U` in `[-1,1]`, one local score or `ABSTAIN` per **unique receiver**, a source-trace digest, and resource counters.

The pairs must be generated by a **separate, eventually trusted** runner that resets identical state and captures true occurrence traces. The offline v0a validator **cannot attest matching physical state, genuine randomization, pre-registration, witness authenticity or independent scoring**. A trial seed is a label, not proof of independence. Reused seeds within the same context, reused trace digests and duplicated receiver judgments are structurally rejected.

No numerical imputation of `ABSTAIN`, unknown global score or missing receiver; no mixing contexts; no pooling the same assessment over convergent provenance paths. Distinct feedback paths do not become independent trials.

## 4. Two diagnostic quantities, without false aggregation

For independent pre-registered trials `k=1,...,n` in context `c`:

`d_k(c) = U_k(p1) - U_k(p0)`.

The module reports their sample mean and a two-sided **exact binomial sign diagnostic** of how frequently the paired differences have the same direction, excluding ties. This p-value is only interpretable under assumptions such as independently sampled trials and a pre-specified test; v0a cannot verify those assumptions, and multiple context/candidate comparisons require multiplicity controls. It is **not** an experimental proof of causal effect or a confidence interval on mean gain.

Local receiver metrics are **not assumed comparable in magnitude across observers**. Instead, within each trial, each receiver with observations in both arms casts only the sign of its own paired change. The trial's local direction is the sign of the majority of these matched receiver directions: `+1`, `0`, `-1`. These signs form **one** sequence across trials for the diagnostic, regardless of how many recipients, paths, or messages exist. An additional versioned calibration and attribution mechanism would be needed before comparing or aggregating raw local magnitudes.

Policy preregisters: a minimum of 8 pairs, direction-diagnostic threshold `alpha_ppm` (at most 100,000), a minimum mean **global** gain, per-arm weighted resource cap, and a maximum allowed mean additional weighted resource cost. Costs are abstract **units**, not watts or joules; every candidate-evaluation operation must be included. Current five counters are operations, memory accesses, spike messages, feedback messages and candidate evaluation operations. This does **not yet** include the complete cost of discovering candidate programs, so an end-to-end optimization claim remains excluded.

## 5. Decision matrix

| Global diagnostic | Local diagnostic | Cost | Research label |
|---|---|---|---|
| Positive, sufficient mean | Any | Within cap | `EVIDENCE_FOR_FURTHER_VERIFICATION` |
| Negative | Positive | Any | `LOCAL_GLOBAL_CONFLICT` |
| Negative | Any other | Any | `GLOBAL_REGRESSION` |
| Missing/insufficient | Any | Any | `GLOBAL_UNAVAILABLE` |
| Positive | Any | Above cap | `COST_BLOCKED` |
| Other | Any | Any | `INCONCLUSIVE` |

**No label triggers a program change.** The source code accepts no approval or promotion field.

## 6. Synthetic adversarial vectors (not evidence about SPK cognition)

The included static JSON contains 60 deliberately constructed pairs, 12 in each of five contexts. It tests the decision mechanism:

1. `beneficial`: positive global and positive local differences, within abstract cost cap.
2. `consensus-trap`: all three recipients prefer the candidate, but independent global task utility is worse. Expected `LOCAL_GLOBAL_CONFLICT`.
3. `ambiguous`: alternating signs. Expected `INCONCLUSIVE`.
4. `global-unavailable`: local feedback present, no global value. Expected `GLOBAL_UNAVAILABLE`.
5. `resource-regression`: global benefit, but cost increase above preregistered cap. Expected `COST_BLOCKED`.

The generated data are **constructed**: neither behavioral adaptability nor causal intervention fidelity is established by passing these tests. Their purpose is to make erroneous inference rules fail.

## 7. Hard boundaries and next integration gates

The reference `spk_backvalidation_v0/contract_v0.py` validates structural occurrence ancestry and trace references. New v0a evidence **does not yet join** the two arms to those occurrence records; both sides only provide opaque trace hashes. An N-node capture adapter must create actual epoch-scoped occurrence IDs, bind the full pre-treatment snapshot and consumed batches, and independently verify the reset, fixed non-target programs and utility scorer. Only then can actual simulator trials be generated.

For the later experimental comparison use five preregistered conditions: fixed F, matched-budget central/random R, local evaluation without propagation L, BV with propagation, and temporally shuffled-feedback negative control S. Use held-out tasks, compare benefits under the same total exploration budget, control for program normal-form equivalence, and account for performance across **all** contexts rather than cherry-picking a favorable context.

**Required follow-up decisions before learning:** honest causal capture, independence of task scorer from local feedback, how to handle overlapping context windows / delayed messages, proper multiplicity and sequential selection corrections, and a full resource accounting policy. Until then, no optimizer, automatic staging, online mutation, or adaptation performance claim.
